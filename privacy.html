Privacy Policy for Kris Wear

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Kris Wear (accessible from kriswear.netlify.app). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Kris Wear and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to register or provide personal identification details just to browse our site. However, if you contact us or interact with forms, we may collect basic details like your name and email address.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Kris Wear follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Kris Wear uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie (Ads)

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

Kris Wear's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
